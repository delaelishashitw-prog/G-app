# Welfare eligibility and defaulters

Members with **three or more unpaid completed calendar months** are labelled **Defaulter — Partially eligible**. They can apply for benefits. The Welfare Committee enters the partial amount during review; the app does not assume a reduction percentage or automatically change existing approvals or payments.

Assessment starts in the recorded membership/joining month. If the joining date is unavailable, the first positive recorded dues month is used and the screen states that basis. Without either date or paid history, the member is marked for review, not assigned invented arrears. Staff should verify historical joining dates and missing receipts before relying on the displayed arrears.

The contribution's applicable `month` determines coverage, even when payment is received later. Positive receipts for the same month count once. Prepayments for future months do not cover earlier gaps. The current month is not overdue. This measures months without a recorded payment; it does not calculate monetary debt or assume a historical monthly dues rate.

Below three unpaid months, the existing three-consecutive-paid-month qualification applies for full benefits; otherwise the member remains on probation for committee review. Three or more unpaid months always produce partial eligibility, including members who have not yet completed that qualification.

Staff can use **Welfare → Member Standing & Balances → Show defaulters**, inspect unpaid months, and export the filtered list. The member portal, applications, claims list, claim review and eligibility calculator use the same policy. External community beneficiaries are assessed separately from member dues.

Tests cover the two/three-month boundary, duplicates, catch-up payments, future months, year boundaries, date fallbacks, the defaulter filter/export, application access, explicit partial approval and portal standing.
