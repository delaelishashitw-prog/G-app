# Welfare eligibility and defaulters

Members with **three or more unpaid completed calendar months** are labelled **Defaulter — Partially eligible**. They can apply for benefits. The Welfare Committee enters the partial amount during review; the app does not assume a reduction percentage or automatically change existing approvals or payments.

The welfare joining date is the earliest recorded payment `date` on a positive welfare dues receipt belonging to that member. Assessment starts in that payment month. Church membership dates do not affect welfare enrollment. The exact welfare joining date appears in staff and member notices and in the standing export. Staff should verify historical payment dates and missing receipts before relying on the displayed arrears.

Members with no positive dated payment are **Not enrolled — No welfare payment**, with no assigned arrears. If a positive receipt has no usable payment date and no dated payment establishes enrollment, the member is marked for payment-date review. Future-dated payments cannot establish enrollment or cover months before their payment date arrives.

After enrollment, the contribution's applicable `month` determines coverage, even when payment is received later. Months before the first payment month are outside the assessment period. Positive receipts for the same month count once. Prepayments for future months do not cover earlier gaps. The current month is not overdue. This measures months without a recorded payment; it does not calculate monetary debt or assume a historical monthly dues rate.

Below three unpaid months, the existing three-consecutive-paid-month qualification applies for full benefits; otherwise the member remains on probation for committee review. Three or more unpaid months always produce partial eligibility, including members who have not yet completed that qualification.

Staff can use **Welfare → Member Standing & Balances → Show defaulters**, inspect unpaid months, and export the filtered list. The member portal, applications, claims list, claim review and eligibility calculator use the same policy. External community beneficiaries are assessed separately from member dues.

Tests cover the two/three-month boundary, duplicates, catch-up payments, future months, year boundaries, first payment versus church membership and applicable month, no-payment enrollment, the defaulter filter/export, application access, explicit partial approval and portal standing.
