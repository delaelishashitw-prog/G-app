# Welfare Officer access

A reviewed super administrator can assign the **Welfare Officer** role through **Users & Roles**. This role requires a verified email, an active login and two-factor authentication. Sign-in opens **Welfare & Relief** directly.

Welfare officers can record member dues, record contributions that are not posted to the treasury, manage appeal events, review relief claims and record committee decisions. Member records are available as context for welfare work, with no member editing permission. The role can read welfare fund receipts and reports, but fund adjustments, receipt voiding, treasury posting and payment processing retain their existing finance/senior staff restrictions.

The database enforces this boundary independently of navigation. Welfare officers cannot read treasury, pastoral cases, staff directories, settings or unrelated modules. Claim payout totals, payment details, beneficiary changes after payment and disbursement status are protected. Treasury-linked appeal receipts require finance staff for corrections. Welfare officers preserve history by closing appeals or declining claims instead of deleting records. New dues and review decisions retain the actual staff identity in their audit trail.

Adding the role does not create an authentication account or send email. A person without a staff login still needs a normal administrator-issued invitation and must complete password setup and two-factor enrollment. Do not describe access as active before that provisioning is verified.
